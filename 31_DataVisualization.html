Jordan has the highest career scoring average (30.1 PPG), while LeBron has the highest rebound (7.5 RPG) and assist (7.4 APG) averages in this snapshot. Kobe averaged 25.0 PPG, 5.2 RPG and 4.7 APG. These statistics describe different contributions and do not measure defense, shot difficulty or era-adjusted value.

Mean series audiences are approximately 25.23 million for Jordan, 16.78 million for Kobe, and 16.24 million for LeBron. These are conditional on Finals appearances and reflect team matchups and television eras. They do not establish how many viewers each player caused to watch.

Player and era are closely connected in these records. The scatterplot helps show this limitation; audience differences cannot be assigned to player identity alone. Keep the valid 2020 bubble observation and examine its influence.

Excluding 2020 raises LeBron’s equal-series mean to about 17.20 million, compared with 16.24 million when all his appearances are included. His mean then exceeds Kobe’s 16.78 million in this dataset. Jordan’s and Kobe’s results are unchanged. This shows why the scope must be stated alongside any ranking.
